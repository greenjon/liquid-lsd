    Shaders License Audit Log

    This document tracks the licensing, authorship, and commercial distribution rights for all Interactive Shader Format (ISF) visual shaders, filters, and generators bundled with Liquid LSD.

    Licensing Policy

    To preserve the right to commercially distribute or monetize Liquid LSD: 1. Permissive Licenses Only: All bundled shaders must use permissive licenses (MIT, BSD-2/3-Clause, Apache 2.0, CC0 / Public Domain) or be original cleanroom implementations. 2. Strictly Prohibited: No shaders under copyleft (GPL/LGPL/AGPL), non-commercial licenses (CC-BY-NC), or all-rights-reserved copyright may be bundled. 3. Attribution Preservation: Copyright notices and original author attributions are preserved in the shader header metadata (CREDIT).
