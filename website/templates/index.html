⚡

Zero-Allocation Audio Thread

Strict real-time audio pipeline avoiding garbage collector pauses. Lock-free ringbuffers transfer spectrum metrics seamlessly to the render loop.

🌀

Parametric Mandala & Feedback

Multi-layered geometric synthesis, polar coordinate mapping, and audio-driven harmonic pulse modulation.

🎛️

Modulation & MIDI Binding

Flexible control-voltage (CV) style matrix allowing audio frequency bands, LFOs, and hardware MIDI knobs to control any visual parameter.

🖥️

Cross-Platform LWJGL & ImGui

Smooth 60+ FPS desktop interface engineered for Linux (x64 / ARM64), macOS (Intel & Apple Silicon), and Windows.

📁

Preset Queues & Playlists

Save and recall visual states instantly. Crossfade morphing between presets for seamless live VJ performance transitions.

📊

Live Telemetry & Diagnostics

Real-time frame time graphs, GC pause trackers, audio xrun counters, and OpenGL pipeline debuggers.
