What it does

Built for Live Sets

🎚️

Four decks, one mix

Run visuals on decks A, B and a background layer, audition the next look on a preview deck, and blend them with a crossfader.

🌀

Generators and shaders

Start from built-in generators like mandalas, spirals and gyroids, or load your own ISF shaders, including ones from Shadertoy and GLSLSandbox.

🎧

Reacts to your audio

Bass, mids and highs, plus beat tracking, drive any parameter. Sync tempo with Ableton Link or tap it in.

✨

Effects and transitions

Stack effect chains on each deck and on the master output, then move between looks with saved transitions.

🎛️

Play it with hardware

A 4×4 macro knob matrix maps to MIDI controllers (with native Midi Fighter Twister support), OSC and TouchOSC. Learn a control with one click.

📁

Presets, playlists and queues

Save any look, build playlists and setlists, and let an auto-VJ queue keep the visuals going between your own moves.

📺

Send it anywhere

Share video with other apps through Spout, Syphon or PipeWire, record your set, or broadcast to a browser-based visualizer.

🆓

Free and open

Released under the GPL-3.0. No account, no subscription, no Java install needed.
